Questions

What does it cost?

Very reasonable rates. Every trip is different, so call me with where and when and I'll give you a price.

Can I pack the car?

Yes. Tell me what's going in when you call and I'll tell you what fits.

Do you bring it back up in the spring?

Yes. Down to Florida in the fall, back up to Jersey when you come home for the summer.

When is a truck carrier the better choice?

If the lowest price matters most, if your dates are wide open, or if the car doesn't run or is a collector car you don't want miles on, a carrier truck is the better fit. I'll tell you that straight when you call.
