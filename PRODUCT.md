# Shamrock Transportation

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (index.html, inline CSS and JS, no frameworks, no tracking, system fonts). Hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users

- **Snowbirds**, mostly 60+, who winter in Florida and summer in New Jersey. They fly down and want their own car waiting at the other end, then reverse it in spring. They find the link on a phone (often inside Facebook's in-app browser) and want to know, fast, who will be driving their car and how to reach him.
- **Edward (Ed) Brannagan**, owner and only known driver. The site is first a gift to him (it has to look like his business, done better than he imagined) and second a way for customers to call him.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for a one-man driveaway service: Ed drives the customer's own car between New Jersey and Florida (and does local, long-distance and Florida airport runs). Success = a snowbird trusts the person behind the wheel enough to tap Call.

## Positioning

The owner is the driver. You know the name of the person driving your car and you have his cell. Because the car is driven, not trucked, it can travel packed with the customer's belongings (inferred from how driveaway works; confirm Ed's limits).

## Operating Context

Seasonal: southbound in fall, northbound in spring (Ellie, 2026-10-13). Customers coordinate flights with the car's arrival; airport pickup/drop-off "anywhere in Florida" is on the card. Contact is by phone (Ed's cell).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Contact: call 732-300-3835 (Ed's cell). Texting: unknown. Email: none. Booking form: none.
- Services, verbatim from the card: "Local & Long Distance", "Airport Services Anywhere In Florida", "Very Reasonable Rates".
- Area: NJ ↔ Florida, plus local and long distance. Exact towns unknown.
- Rates: $1 per mile (Ed, via Ellie, 2026-10-15). What it includes is unknown.
- Undecided / unknown: insurance, driver vetting, hours, years in business, reviews.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Shamrock Transportation. Mark: a green shamrock (business card). The card itself is not cleared for public use yet.
- Card tagline, verbatim: "Life is a Highway... Drive it All Night Long".
- Domain on the card: youflyilldrive.com ("You fly, I'll drive"), currently unregistered.
- Voice: plain-spoken, direct, neighborly; first person singular (Ed).

## Evidence on Hand

Business-card facts only (via Muse brief 2026-10-13). No photos, no logo file, no reviews, no social accounts, no Facebook. None of these may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Ed is the product: name, face (when we get one) and cell number up front.
2. Only what's true. Unknowns stay off the page, never softened into claims.
3. Honest objection handling (say when a truck carrier is the better choice) builds more trust than selling.
4. One tap to call, everywhere, for an older audience on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience skews 60+: body text 18px, large tap targets (56px buttons), high contrast, nothing that depends on hover or fine motor control.
