# Design System: Shamrock Transportation

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Drive Down I-95"**

The whole page is one drive down I-95. It opens at the roadside at dusk: a green interstate guide sign stands on two steel posts above the road, under a sky fading up from night blue, and interstate green is also shamrock green, so the mark and the road share one colour. The driver gets his own sign in the blue of an I-95 services sign, so the two signs never repeat. The light "sign-back aluminum" run reads like the back of a road sign: cool, matte, plain. The close is the highway after dark: blue-green, never black, with the car driving it. Headlight amber is the only warm light on the page. At rest it belongs to calling and to the road's centre line, and nothing else in the interface; the dusk glow on the hero's horizon is scenery.

The page is calm and plainly structured. Its readers are mostly 40 to 70, on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. After a UX research brief for that audience, Ellie kept the I-95 world and adopted a plainer structure. On 2026-10-15 she asked for more colour, which turned the driver sign blue and put the hero on a green guide sign, which now stands in a still roadside scene at dusk. The hero sign holds a large sign-white headline, a one-line promise, one wide Call button with the number written under "Call for a free quote", a plain "or send a text" link, and the owner's name beside a slot for his photo. Then come the blue meet-the-driver sign, three numbered steps, plain questions including cost, and a close that opens on the road band with the car driving it, then a big phone number. On phones a small Call pill is never far away. The car is the one moving thing on the page. The road is scenery to watch, not a toy: it takes no touch, the page scrolls straight over it, and nothing on the page asks to be tapped open. Body text is 18px, buttons are 58px tall (the hero Call is 66px), every word is visible on arrival, and nothing depends on hover.

The world rejects the trucking-company template: no fleet photos, no quote forms, no badges. Type is the system font stack set heavy (700–850), which is Ellie's pitch-site house style. Section headlines are uppercase; the hero headline is mixed case.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two interstate signs carry the colour: the hero is a green guide sign on two posts above a road at dusk, and Meet your driver is a blue services sign. The header sits on night above the scene; then come one long cool-aluminum run and a blue-green night close. No pure black anywhere as a surface.
- Every sign face is drawn the same way: a 3px sign-white inset border on a full field of colour, rounded.
- One warm colour, amber, reserved at rest for calling and for the centre line.
- Shield blue appears only when something is hovered or focused; services-sign blue is only ever a ground.
- Three photo slots wait for Ed: a wide photo in the desktop hero, a round face beside the owner line on phones, a portrait on the driver sign.
- Only the car in the close moves. The hero's roadside scene, seams, sections and text are still.
- Everything is visible: no accordions, no tabs, no tap-to-reveal.
- The phone number is always written out, and on phones a small Call pill is never far away.
- Heavy system sans with big tap targets, built for older eyes and thumbs.

## Colors

The palette is an interstate: guide-sign green, services-sign blue, a dusk sky, cool aluminum, a blue-green night ground, one headlight amber, and a shield blue that appears only on interaction.

### Primary
- **Interstate Guide Green** (sign): the hero guide sign, green inside its sign-white border and again as a 10px rim outside it, like a real guide sign. Also the state-line signs drawn on the canvas, the numbered discs in How it works, and the small square bullets in What I do. It is also shamrock green. Use it for signage and wayfinding marks, never as a button fill.
- **Sign Edge Green** (sign-deep): the ground of the two hero photo slots (the wide desktop photo and the round phone face), and the 1px outer edge of the hero sign's green rim. It also draws the roof-box straps in the trunk illustration.
- **Services Sign Blue** (svc): the field of the Meet your driver sign, after the blue I-95 services signs. It is a ground at rest, not an interaction colour, and it appears nowhere else.
- **Services Edge Blue** (svc-deep): the driver sign's 2px bottom edge, which gives it metal thickness, and the ground of the portrait photo slot.
- **Sign White** (sign-white): legend colour on both signs: the hero headline, the hero's 3px inset border, the clover in the favicon, the owner line, the driver sign's inset border, headline, lede and number (its underline at 50%, sign-white-50), the step numbers, focus rings in the header and on both signs, and canvas road-edge lines (80% alpha) and reflector dots (30%). At 60% (sign-white-60) it is the dashed border of every photo slot; at full strength, their icon and caption. It is a raw literal in the source, not a `:root` property.

### Secondary
- **Headlight Amber** (amber): at rest, the fill and border of every Call button and the Call pill, the road canvas centre line (dashed 16/16), the lane line on both seams, and `::selection`. On hover, the underline of the hero text link and the driver-sign number. In the hero scene it is also the road's centre dashes and the warm end of the horizon glow (42%). Text on amber is always ink.
- **Lit Amber** (amber-hover): the Call button's hover fill, under `(hover:hover)` only.

### Tertiary
- **Interstate Shield Blue** (focus): hover borders and underlines on the Call buttons (the hero's included) and the Text button, the close number, footer links; `:focus-visible` outlines on night.
- **Deep Shield Blue** (focus-deep): the focus role on aluminum, dark enough for 3:1 against paper.
- **Shamrock Leaf** (leaf): the clover in the header lockup only. Not an interface colour.

### Neutral
- **I-95 Night** (night): the page ground (the header sits on it), the top of the hero sky, and the close, where the road band runs. The same value is **Ink** (ink): all text on aluminum (headlines, ledes, steps, services, questions and answers) and text on amber. There is no secondary grey text on light grounds. **Night Clear** (night-0) is night at zero alpha, the top stop of the fade that dissolves the road band into the close.
- **Dusk Sky** (night, dusk-mid, dusk-low): the hero's vertical sky gradient behind the sign: night at the top, dusk-mid at 55%, dusk-low at the horizon. Used nowhere else.
- **Night Shoulder** (night-2): footer ground, one step lighter than the page.
- **Sign-back Aluminum** (paper): the one light ground. Meet your driver, How it works / What I do, Driven not trucked, and Questions sit on one continuous aluminum run.
- **Headlight Haze** (on-dark): text on the green sign (the promise, the hero text link) and on night (header nav and phone, close headline and number, footer name and link). At 55% (on-dark-55) it is the Text button's outline; at 40% (on-dark-40) it is the close number's underline. **Dim Haze** (on-dark-soft): secondary text on night (the closing lede, the tagline, the footer lines).
- **White** (white): the Text button's label on hover only.
- **Guardrail** (rule): solid 2px rules framing the question pairs. **Guardrail Dotted** (rule-dotted): 2px dotted rules between services.
- **Road Band** (road-band to road-band-2): the vertical gradient behind the canvas, visible before the first frame paints. Road-band is also the fill of the seam that leads into the close, so the lane line runs straight onto the road.
- **Shadow tints** (shadow-sign, shadow-pill): the only two shadow colours. Black at 55% under the sign; a night-tinted 55% under the Call pill.

### Named Rules
**The One Headlight Rule.** At rest, amber is used only for calling (the Call buttons and the Call pill) and the road centre line (the canvas centre dash, the centre dashes of the hero road, and the lane line on the seams). Its one other interface job is a response: the hero text link and the driver-sign number take an amber hover underline, because they sit on the signs, where shield blue would sink into the green and the services blue. Links on night, the header included, hover in shield blue. Don't use amber at rest for links, borders, icons, headings, or a non-call button. Outside the interface amber appears only as scenery: the warm end of the dusk glow behind the hero's posts, and the cargo in the two car drawings, which is what "packed" looks like at that size.

**The Shield Blue Only Responds Rule.** The page has two blues with opposite jobs. Interstate-shield blue (focus, focus-deep) appears only as a response to the pointer or keyboard: hover borders on the Call and Text buttons, hover underlines on night, focus outlines on night and aluminum. It is never at rest. Services-sign blue (svc, svc-deep) is a ground: the Meet your driver sign and its portrait slot, always at rest, never a hover, focus, link or button colour. Don't let one stand in for the other.

**The Never Black Rule.** Dark grounds are blue-green night (night, night-2), guide green or services blue, never #000. Black appears only as low-alpha shadow under the driver sign and inside the canvas; the hero sign's shadow is night-tinted.

## Typography

**Display Font:** System UI stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** None. There is one family.

**Character:** One family, set heavy. Weight 850 for headlines and phone numbers, 700–800 for titles, questions, links and buttons, 400 for reading text. It reads like highway lettering. The stack is Ellie's pitch-site house style (no webfont request); Roboto is in it only as the Android fallback.

- **Display** (850, clamp(3rem, 14vw, 5.2rem), 1, -0.02em; **Display Wide** clamp(3rem, 6vw, 4.4rem) from 760px, so it fits the 880px sign): mixed case, sign-white on the green hero sign. Only the hero headline ("You fly. / I'll drive."), one phrase per line.

### Named Rules
**The System Stack Rule.** Ellie's house style: the native system sans and nothing else on this site. Hierarchy comes from weight, size, and case.

**The Number Is Written Out Rule.** Wherever the page asks someone to call, the number 732-300-3835 is visible in text: the header (desktop), the second line of every Call button, the driver sign, the close, and the footer. The Call pill is the one place it lives only in the accessible label, because the pill must stay small.

## Layout

A single column of full-bleed bands inside a 1120px container with a 20px gutter. The page runs, top to bottom: the header on night; the hero, a roadside scene at dusk with the green guide sign on its posts; a seam onto aluminum; Meet your driver (the blue services sign); How it works beside What I do; Driven, not trucked; Questions; a seam onto the road; the close, which opens with the road band and then carries the headline, lede, phone number and buttons; the footer. One roadside sign, one light run, one dark close.

The header sits on night and pads 14px 20px 4px. The hero below it is a still roadside scene: a sky gradient, the guide sign, its posts, and the road. The sign starts 30px below the header (48px at 760px and up). Its face is a sign-green panel with a 3px sign-white border at 16px radius, 34px in from each screen edge on phones (padding 24/18/28), capped at 880px and centred from 760px (padding 40/44/44); the 10px green rim and 1px edge sit outside the border. Inside the face is one left-aligned column, each child capped at 680px: headline, promise (20px gap), then the call group (6px above, 6px inside: the wide Call button, then the text link), then the owner line with its round face slot. At 960px and up the face splits into two equal columns 56px apart: copy on the left, the wide photo slot on the right spanning the copy's rows and centred against it; the round face slot steps aside. Under the sign, 11px down to clear the rim, the posts band is 64px tall (96px from 760px): two steel posts (10px wide, 14px from 760px) at 30% and 70%, a treeline along its foot (34px tall, 52px from 760px), and a warm horizon glow behind them. Then the ground, 150px (200px from 760px): grass, a road in perspective whose edge lines start at the foot of the posts and widen past the screen edges, and amber centre dashes that grow toward the viewer. The seam onto aluminum overlaps the foot of the road by its own height (-44px, -64px from 760px).

On phones everything else stacks. At 760px and up:
- the header adds a three-link section nav (Meet your driver / How it works / Questions) and the phone number, 22px apart, with the brand lockup pushed left
- How it works and What I do sit side by side, 1fr / 1fr with a 64px gap
- Driven, not trucked splits 1.1fr / 1fr, copy beside the trunk drawing
- the driver sign caps at 960px wide, with the portrait slot in a 240px column beside the text (40px gap) instead of above it
- the close's buttons sit side by side at their own width
- the Call pill is removed
- the hero sign caps at 880px, the headline steps down to Display Wide, and the posts and road grow
- at 960px, the hero sign face splits into copy and wide photo

Questions cap at 760px wide so answers keep a readable measure on desktop.

Section padding is 44px top / 64px bottom on phones and 56 / 88 at 760px and up. Blocks that follow another block on the same ground close up to 8px at the top, so the aluminum run reads as one surface. The close has no top padding: the road band sits flush under its seam, full bleed, 190px tall on phones and 260px from 760px, and a 40px gradient at its foot dissolves it into night. The close copy starts 36px below and the section ends 56px down. The footer carries 104px of bottom padding on phones so the Call pill never covers its last line (40px at 760px and up). The default vertical gap inside a section is 22px (lede margin 18px, close number 18px below the lede, buttons 22px below it).

Breakpoints:
- **760px** shows the header nav and phone, switches to the split layouts, hides the Call pill
- **960px** splits the hero sign face into copy and the wide photo slot, and hides the round face slot
- **canvas under 700px wide** narrows the road (60px instead of 78px) and the canvas sign type
- **`(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`** hides every Send a text control, since `sms:` does nothing on most desktops

### Named Rules
**The Two Buttons Max Rule.** A contact group holds at most two controls: Call (amber, stacked "Call for a free quote" over the number), then text. The hero pairs the wide Call with a plain "or send a text" link; the close pairs Call with a Send a text outline button. Don't add a third channel or a duplicate to a group.

**The No Sticky Bar, One Small Pill Rule.** No full-width sticky or fixed bars or banners, ever. A single small labeled Call pill is allowed on phones only: amber, fully rounded, "Call Ed", pinned 16px from the bottom-right corner (plus the safe-area inset). It shows only after the hero call group has scrolled away and hides while the closing buttons are on screen, so it never doubles a visible Call. This is a deliberate per-site exception Ellie made on 2026-10-14 for this 40–70 audience; it does not carry to other sites.

**The Nothing Hidden Behind A Tap Rule.** No accordions, tabs, carousels, "read more" or tap-to-reveal. Every question shows its answer in full and every step shows its text. If text is worth having on the page, it is visible on arrival; if it isn't, cut it.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat except for its two signs and the pill. Aluminum and night sections use no shadows: depth comes from the change of ground and from the seams. Both signs are lifted because a real sign stands off its post: the hero sign hangs over its posts and road on a long night-tinted drop, and the driver sign stands off aluminum. The Call pill is lifted because it floats over the page. Inside the road canvas, the car, trees, houses and state signs cast small offset shadows at 28–35% black. That is the canvas's own top-down lighting, not UI elevation.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sign Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.55), 0 2px 0 #0f3c6c` on the services sign): sign panels only. The diffuse part lifts the panel off the ground. The 2px lip is the panel's own metal edge, always the sign's own deep tone (svc-deep on the blue sign, sign-deep on a green panel) and never black.
- **Roadside Sign** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 10px #0a6337, 0 0 0 11px #084d2b, 0 22px 40px -10px rgba(5,15,25,.6)`): the hero sign only. The first two layers are not elevation: they paint the 10px green rim outside the white border and its 1px sign-deep metal edge. The third is a night-tinted drop that lifts the sign off the dusk sky.
- **Pill Float** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -6px rgba(5,20,25,.55)`): the Call pill only, a night-tinted diffuse shadow that separates it from whatever scrolls beneath.

### Named Rules
**The Only Signs And The Pill Stand Up Rule.** Only sign panels and the floating Call pill get elevation. Buttons, links, step discs, lists, question pairs and sections stay flat.

## Shapes

Corners are soft. The services sign is 16px outside and 11px on the inset; the inset is a 3px sign-white border set 7px in from the panel edge, so the two radii stay concentric. The hero sign's face is a 3px sign-white border at 16px on green; its 10px green rim and 1px edge are spread shadows, so they follow the same curve outward. The hero posts are plain square-ended steel. Photo slots are 12px (the wide hero photo), 8px (the portrait), or a full circle (the face). Buttons are 12px. The brand link is 8px, so its focus ring hugs the lockup. Focus rings are 6px. Service bullets are 12px squares at 3px. Step numbers sit in 44px circles. The Call pill is the only fully rounded control (999px), which marks it as a different object from the in-page buttons. Section edges are never straight lines: each change of ground is a wavy edge drawn by a seam.

## Components

### Buttons
Large, direct, and only for the thumb.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (12px), 58px minimum height, 2px border, a 22px inline SVG icon (phone or chat) 10px before the label, no wrapping.
- **Call (primary):** amber fill and border, ink text. The label is two lines, left-aligned: "Call for a free quote" in Button, the number beneath in Button Number. In the close it fills the column on phones and sizes to content from 760px.
- **Call, wide (hero):** the same button made bigger for the first viewport: 66px minimum height, 22px side padding, content aligned left.
- **Send a text (ghost):** transparent fill, on-dark text, 2px border at on-dark-55. Opens `sms:` with a pre-filled message. Close only. Hidden on fine-pointer devices.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover applies only under `(hover:hover)`. Call lightens to lit amber and its border turns shield blue. The ghost's border turns shield blue and its text goes white. Focus is a 3px outline at 3px offset: shield blue on night, sign-white in the header and on both signs. Transitions: background and border .2s, transform .15s; pressing nudges the button down 1px.

### Text Link
- **Or send a text (hero):** Text Link type in on-dark with a 2px underline at 5px offset, 48px minimum height, 4px side padding, left-aligned under the Call button. The underline turns amber on hover; focus is sign-white. Opens the same pre-filled `sms:`. Hidden on fine-pointer devices.

### Call Pill
The one floating element, phones only.
- **Shape:** fully rounded (999px), 56px minimum height, 20px side padding, a 20px phone icon 8px before "Call Ed" in Call Pill type. Its accessible name carries the full "Call Ed for a free quote, 732-300-3835".
- **Colour:** amber fill, ink text, Pill Float shadow.
- **Behaviour:** fixed bottom-right, 16px from the edges plus the bottom safe area. Hidden (transparent, 12px lower, not tappable) until the hero call group scrolls above the viewport, and again while the closing buttons are visible. It appears and leaves with a .25s opacity and position transition, which is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Gone at 760px and up.

### Signs (signature)
The house component: a field of colour with a 3px sign-white inset border and sign-white legend. Two signs are on the page, and they never share a colour.
- **Hero guide sign (green, roadside):** a sign-green panel with a 3px sign-white border at 16px radius, a 10px green rim and 1px sign-deep edge outside the border, and the Roadside Sign drop, standing on two steel posts above a road in perspective at dusk (sizes in Layout). The hero text link takes an amber hover underline; every focus outline in the hero is sign-white. The whole scene is still.
- **Services sign (blue), "Meet your driver":** a services-blue panel (7px padding, 16px radius, Sign Lift with a services-edge lip) on aluminum, an inset with a 3px sign-white border at 11px radius, content in sign-white. Inset padding 24/20/26 on phones, 40/44 wide, panel capped at 960px. The inset is a grid, 22px gap: the portrait slot above the text on phones, beside it (240px / 1fr, 40px gap, centred) from 760px. The text holds an uppercase Headline, a Lede in sign-white, and the Phone Number link: a 3px underline at sign-white-50 that turns amber on hover, a 30px phone icon, 48px minimum height. Focus outlines inside the sign are sign-white.
- **Canvas variant:** the state-line signs drawn in the road ("Welcome to / Georgia"): guide green, a 1.5px sign-white inset stroke, 4px radius, Canvas Sign type, mixed case.

### Photo Slots
Three fixed places for Ed's own photos. The page names him, so it shows him; it never shows stock or fleet photography. Until his photos arrive each slot holds a placeholder (dashed 2px sign-white-60 border, a person icon in sign-white, and on the two larger slots a 16px/700 caption). The slot's shape, size and ground are the system; the dashed placeholder is not, and each one is swapped for an `<img>` (object-fit: cover, filling the slot) from the HTML comment above it.
- **Wide hero photo** (`.hero-photo`): desktop only (960px and up), the right column of the hero sign face, full column width, 4:3, 12px radius, sign-deep ground. Placeholder icon 72px at 80% opacity, 12px above the caption.
- **Owner face** (`.owner-face`): phones and tablets (under 960px), a 56px circle 14px before the owner line, sign-deep ground. Placeholder icon 26px.
- **Driver portrait** (`.ed-photo`): on the services sign, 4:5, 8px radius, svc-deep ground; min(200px, 60%) wide on phones, the full 240px column from 760px. Placeholder icon 56px at 80% opacity, 10px above the caption.

### Steps (How it works)
Three static numbered steps, never a carousel or a timeline animation.
- **Structure:** an ordered list, 22px apart. Each step is a 48px column holding a 44px sign-green disc with its sign-white Step Number, beside a Question-style h3 and a body paragraph (max 46ch).

### Seams (signature)
A still lane line along the top edge of the next ground.
- **Form:** an SVG with a 1200×64 viewBox stretched full width, 44px tall (64px at 760px and up), sitting in flow between bands. The fill path takes the next ground (`--next`): paper over the foot of the hero road, which it overlaps by its own height, road-band above the close. The stroke is a 3px dashed lane line (26 on, 22 off, round caps) in `--lane`.
- **Wave:** `y = 34 + 9·sin(x/190 + φ) + 5·sin(x/83 + 2φ)`, sampled every 20 units, with φ = 1.7 × seam index so no two seams match. Drawn once on load; it never animates.
- **Colour:** amber lane on both seams: the first leaves the hero road for aluminum, the second leaves aluminum for the road.

### Road Canvas (signature)
A top-down night highway band that opens the close: scenery to watch, never a control.
- **Scene:** a winding two-lane road across eight states (New Jersey to Florida, 900px of road each). Ground warms from cool green in Jersey to warmer green in Florida. Shoulder gravel, asphalt, sign-white edge lines at 80%, an amber centre dash 16/16, still reflector dots at 30% sign-white. Trees go pine to oak to palm on the way south. A house sits at each end of the trip. The packed cream car (amber and green cargo) carries an additive headlight beam. A 40px night fade covers its foot.
- **Motion:** the car drives at a steady CRUISE of 110px/s from one house to the other, pauses 2.5s, turns round and drives back. The camera and lane position ease in; nothing else in the scene moves. Direction starts southbound September to February and northbound March to August.
- **Input:** none. No drag, no steering, no hint, no `touch-action` override: the page scrolls normally over the band.
- **Running:** one shared requestAnimationFrame loop (frame time capped at 50ms), paused whenever the band is off screen. Road width 60px when the canvas is under 700px wide, 78px otherwise; devicePixelRatio capped at 2.
- **Reduced motion:** draws one still frame.

**The One Moving Thing Rule.** Only the car in the close moves. The hero roadside scene (sky, sign, posts, treeline, road), seams, text, illustrations and sections never animate, reveal on scroll, or loop. The only other changes on the page are state feedback (colour shifts and a 1px press) in direct response to a touch, hover or keyboard focus, and the Call pill's short fade in and out, which is switched off under reduced motion.

### Lists
- **What I do:** Title-style lines between 2px dotted guardrail rules, each led by a 12px sign-green square (3px radius).
- **Questions:** plain question/answer pairs in a block capped at 760px: a Question h3 (20px/750) above a body paragraph, with a 2px solid guardrail rule above the first pair and below every answer. Cost is answered first, with the rate stated plainly: $1 per mile, the same figure as How it works step one and the close lede. Always open; no toggle, no icon.

### Navigation
- **Header:** not sticky, on night (14px 20px 4px padding). The brand lockup is a link back to the top: a 34px leaf-green clover and "Shamrock Transportation" in Wordmark, 8px radius for its focus ring. On phones that is all the header holds. Focus outlines in the header are sign-white.
- **Desktop (760px and up):** three in-page links (Meet your driver / How it works / Questions) in Nav Link type, then the phone number in Bar Phone type with a 20px phone icon, all on-dark, 44px minimum height. Hover adds a 2px shield-blue bottom border, as on every other link on night; at rest the border is transparent.

### Close and Footer
- **Close:** on night, under the road band. Headline (seasonal: "Heading south this winter?" / "Heading home this spring?"), a lede in dim haze, the Close Number in on-dark with a 3px on-dark-40 underline that turns shield blue on hover, the contact pair (max 520px), then the card tagline in italic body.
- **Footer:** night shoulder ground, 16px dim haze lines: the name in on-dark at 18px, the owner line, the service-area line ("New Jersey and Florida, door to door"), and the phone link. Links have a 2px underline at 4px offset, 44px minimum height, and take a shield-blue underline on hover. Four columns spread apart at 760px and up.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** sign the page like I-95: the hero as a green guide sign on two posts above the road, with a 3px sign-white face border at 16px and a 10px green rim outside it, and the driver on a blue services sign (16/11px concentric radii, Sign Lift with a services-edge lip).
- **Do** keep amber at rest for calling and the centre line; the hero text link and driver-sign number take an amber hover underline (they sit on the green and blue signs); links on night hover in shield blue. Text on amber is always ink.
- **Do** write the phone number out wherever the page asks for a call, and put it on the second line of every Call button.
- **Do** show shield blue only on hover (gated behind `(hover:hover)`) and focus. Use deep shield blue on aluminum and sign-white focus on both signs.
- **Do** keep each photo slot's shape when Ed's photo lands: 4:3 at 12px for the wide hero photo, a 56px circle for the face, 4:5 at 8px for the portrait, image set to cover.
- **Do** stitch every change of ground with a still seam whose fill is the next ground's colour.
- **Do** keep reading text at 18px or larger and buttons at 58px tall or more (the Call pill at 56px).
- **Do** show every answer and every step in full, on arrival.
- **Do** keep the car the only moving thing: run it on the shared loop, pause it off screen, and draw a still frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** write icons as inline SVG symbols that inherit `currentColor`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second moving thing: no rolling seams, scroll reveals, marquees, looping illustrations, or extra traffic in the road.
- **Don't** make the road band interactive or capture touch on it. It is scenery; the page must scroll over it.
- **Don't** hide text behind a tap: no accordions, tabs, carousels or "read more".
- **Don't** add a full-width sticky or fixed bar or banner, ever. The one allowed floating element is the small labeled Call pill on phones.
- **Don't** put more than two contact controls in a group, or use amber at rest for anything that isn't calling.
- **Don't** use shield blue at rest as a link colour, accent, or fill, and don't use services blue for anything but the driver sign's ground.
- **Don't** give both signs the same colour, or animate anything in the hero scene: the sign, posts, treeline and road are a still drawing.
- **Don't** hand the site over with a dashed placeholder still in a photo slot.
- **Don't** use #000 or near-black as a ground. Night is blue-green.
- **Don't** put a tracked uppercase label above a headline. The page has no tracked uppercase text.
- **Don't** add shadows to buttons, links, step discs, lists, question pairs or sections. Only signs and the Call pill stand up.
- **Don't** use stock fleet photos, quote forms, or trust badges.
