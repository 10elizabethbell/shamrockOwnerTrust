# Design System: Shamrock Transportation

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Drive Down I-95"**

The whole page is one drive south at night. The ground is the highway after dark: blue-green, never black. The driver goes up on an interstate guide sign, and interstate green is also shamrock green, so the mark and the road share one colour. Headlight amber is the only warm light on the page. It belongs to calling and to the road's centre line, and nothing else in the interface. The light "sign-back aluminum" run reads like the back of a road sign: cool, matte, plain.

The page is calm and plainly structured. Its readers are mostly 40 to 70, on a phone, often inside Facebook's in-app browser. After a UX research brief for that audience, Ellie kept the night-drive world and adopted a plainer structure. The hero is a quiet night: a large sign-white headline, a one-line promise, one wide Call button with the number written under "Call for a free quote", a plain "or send a text" link, and the owner's name. Then come a meet-the-driver sign, three numbered steps, plain questions including cost, and a close that opens on the road band with the car driving it, then a big phone number. On phones a small Call pill is never far away. The car is the one moving thing on the page. The road is scenery to watch, not a toy: it takes no touch, the page scrolls straight over it, and nothing on the page asks to be tapped open. Body text is 18px, buttons are 58px tall (the hero Call is 66px), every word is visible on arrival, and nothing depends on hover.

The world rejects the trucking-company template: no fleet photos, no quote forms, no badges. Type is the system font stack set heavy (700–850), which is Ellie's pitch-site house style. Section headlines are uppercase; the hero headline is mixed case.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Blue-green night ground with one long cool-aluminum light run. No pure black anywhere as a surface.
- One guide-sign panel (green, white inset border, rounded) carries the driver introduction.
- One warm colour, amber, reserved for calling and for the centre line.
- Interstate-shield blue appears only when something is hovered or focused.
- Only the car moves. Seams, sections and text are still.
- Everything is visible: no accordions, no tabs, no tap-to-reveal.
- The phone number is always written out, and on phones a small Call pill is never far away.
- Heavy system sans with big tap targets, built for older eyes and thumbs.

## Colors

The palette is a night highway: a blue-green ground, sign green, cool aluminum, one headlight amber, and a shield blue that appears only on interaction.

### Primary
- **Interstate Guide Green** (sign): the field of the Meet your driver sign and the state-line signs drawn on the canvas, the numbered discs in How it works, and the small square bullets in What I do. It is also shamrock green. Use it for signage and wayfinding marks, never as a button fill.
- **Sign Edge Green** (sign-deep): the sign panel's 2px bottom edge, which gives it metal thickness. It also draws the roof-rack straps in the trunk illustration.
- **Sign White** (sign-white): the hero headline on night, and legend colour on green: the 3px inset border, sign lettering, the step numbers, the phone number on the driver sign (its underline at 50%, sign-white-50), focus rings inside the sign, and canvas road-edge lines (80% alpha) and reflector dots (30%). It is a raw literal in the source, not a `:root` property.

### Secondary
- **Headlight Amber** (amber): the fill and border of every Call button and the Call pill, the road canvas centre line (dashed 16/16), the lane line on both seams, and `::selection`. Text on amber is always ink.
- **Lit Amber** (amber-hover): the Call button's hover fill, under `(hover:hover)` only.

### Tertiary
- **Interstate Shield Blue** (focus): hover borders and underlines and `:focus-visible` outlines on dark grounds: Call and Text buttons, the hero text link, header nav links and header phone, the driver-sign number, the close number, footer links.
- **Deep Shield Blue** (focus-deep): the same role on aluminum, dark enough for 3:1 against paper.
- **Shamrock Leaf** (leaf): the clover mark only (brand lockup and favicon). Not an interface colour.

### Neutral
- **I-95 Night** (night): page ground, header, hero and close. The same value is **Ink** (ink): all text on aluminum (headlines, ledes, steps, services, questions and answers) and text on amber. There is no secondary grey text on light grounds. **Night Clear** (night-0) is night at zero alpha, the top stop of the fade that dissolves the road band into the close.
- **Night Shoulder** (night-2): footer ground, one step lighter than the page.
- **Sign-back Aluminum** (paper): the one light ground. Meet your driver, How it works / What I do, Driven not trucked, and Questions sit on one continuous aluminum run.
- **Headlight Haze** (on-dark): text on night: the promise, the hero text link, header nav and phone, close number, footer name and link. At 55% (on-dark-55) it is the Text button's outline; at 40% (on-dark-40) it is the close number's underline. **Dim Haze** (on-dark-soft): secondary text on night (the hero owner line, the closing lede, the tagline, the footer lines).
- **White** (white): the Text button's label on hover only.
- **Guardrail** (rule): solid 2px rules framing the question pairs. **Guardrail Dotted** (rule-dotted): 2px dotted rules between services.
- **Road Band** (road-band to road-band-2): the vertical gradient behind the canvas, visible before the first frame paints. Road-band is also the fill of the seam that leads into the close, so the lane line runs straight onto the road.
- **Shadow tints** (shadow-sign, shadow-pill): the only two shadow colours. Black at 55% under the sign; a night-tinted 55% under the Call pill.

### Named Rules
**The One Headlight Rule.** In the interface, amber is used only for calling (the Call buttons and the Call pill) and the road centre line (the canvas centre dash and the lane line on the seams). Don't use it for links, borders, icons, headings, or a non-call button. The only amber outside the interface is the cargo in the two car drawings, which is what "packed" looks like at that size.

**The Blue Only Responds Rule.** Interstate-shield blue appears only as a response to the pointer or keyboard: hover borders and underlines, focus outlines. It is never at rest.

**The Never Black Rule.** Dark grounds are blue-green night (night, night-2), never #000. Black appears only as low-alpha shadow under the sign and inside the canvas.

## Typography

**Display Font:** System UI stack (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Roboto, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Same stack
**Label/Mono Font:** None. There is one family.

**Character:** One family, set heavy. Weight 850 for headlines and phone numbers, 700–800 for titles, questions, links and buttons, 400 for reading text. It reads like highway lettering. The stack is Ellie's pitch-site house style (no webfont request); Roboto is in it only as the Android fallback.

- **Display** (850, clamp(3rem, 14vw, 5.2rem), 1, -0.02em): mixed case, sign-white on night. Only the hero headline ("You fly. / I'll drive."), one phrase per line.

- **Close Number** (850, clamp(30px, 9vw, 44px), -0.01em): the phone number in the close, set above the buttons as the biggest number on the page.
- **Phone Number** (850, clamp(26px, 8vw, 40px), -0.01em): the cell number on the driver sign.
- **Title** (750, clamp(21px, 5.6vw, 26px), 1.35): What I do list lines.
- **Question** (750, 20px, 1.35, no tracking): the h3 of each question pair and of each How it works step.
- **Step Number** (850, 20px): the numeral inside each step disc.
- **Lede** (400, clamp(18px, 4.8vw, 21px), 1.5, max 38ch): the paragraph under a section headline, 18px below it.
- **Promise** (400, clamp(19px, 5.2vw, 22px), 1.5, max 30ch): the one-line service promise under the hero headline.
- **Body** (400, 18px, 1.55): answers, step text (max 46ch), and the closing tagline (italic, dim haze). 18px is the floor for reading text.
- **Button** (800, 18px): Call and Text labels. **Button Number** (700, 16px, 85% opacity): the number on the second line of each Call button.
- **Text Link** (700, 18px): "or send a text" under the hero Call.
- **Owner Line** (400, 17px): "Owner & driver: Ed Brannagan" in dim haze at the foot of the hero.
- **Call Pill** (850, 18px): "Call Ed".
- **Nav Link** (700, 17px) and **Bar Phone** (850, 20px): the desktop header.
- **Wordmark** (800, 17px, 0.01em): "Shamrock Transportation" beside the clover.
- **Small** (400, 16px): footer lines. The footer's name steps back up to 18px.
- **Canvas Sign** (700, 13.5px; 12.5px when the canvas is under 700px wide): state-line sign lettering drawn in the road.

### Named Rules
**The System Stack Rule.** Ellie's house style: the native system sans and nothing else on this site. Hierarchy comes from weight, size, and case.

**The Number Is Written Out Rule.** Wherever the page asks someone to call, the number 732-300-3835 is visible in text: the header (desktop), the second line of every Call button, the driver sign, the close, and the footer. The Call pill is the one place it lives only in the accessible label, because the pill must stay small.

## Layout

A single column of full-bleed bands inside a 1120px container with a 20px gutter. The page runs, top to bottom: header; the quiet-night hero; a seam into aluminum; Meet your driver (the driver sign); How it works beside What I do; Driven, not trucked; Questions; a seam onto the road; the close, which opens with the road band and then carries the headline, lede, phone number and buttons; the footer. One light run, one dark close.

The hero is one left-aligned column at every width, each child capped at 680px: headline, promise (20px gap), then the call group (6px above, 6px inside: the wide Call button, then the text link), then the owner line. Padding is 34px top / 28px bottom on phones and 56 / 44 at 760px and up.

On phones everything else stacks. At 760px and up:
- the header adds a three-link section nav (Meet your driver / How it works / Questions) and the phone number, 22px apart, with the brand lockup pushed left
- How it works and What I do sit side by side, 1fr / 1fr with a 64px gap
- Driven, not trucked splits 1.1fr / 1fr, copy beside the trunk drawing
- the driver sign caps at 860px wide
- the close's buttons sit side by side at their own width
- the Call pill is removed

Questions cap at 760px wide so answers keep a readable measure on desktop.

Section padding is 44px top / 64px bottom on phones and 56 / 88 at 760px and up. Blocks that follow another block on the same ground close up to 8px at the top, so the aluminum run reads as one surface. The close has no top padding: the road band sits flush under its seam, full bleed, 190px tall on phones and 260px from 760px, and a 40px gradient at its foot dissolves it into night. The close copy starts 36px below and the section ends 56px down. The footer carries 104px of bottom padding on phones so the Call pill never covers its last line (40px at 760px and up). The default vertical gap inside a section is 22px (lede margin 18px, close number 18px below the lede, buttons 22px below it).

Breakpoints:
- **760px** shows the header nav and phone, switches to the split layouts, hides the Call pill
- **canvas under 700px wide** narrows the road (60px instead of 78px) and the canvas sign type
- **`(hover:hover) and (pointer:fine)`** hides every Send a text control, since `sms:` does nothing on most desktops

### Named Rules
**The Two Buttons Max Rule.** A contact group holds at most two controls: Call (amber, stacked "Call for a free quote" over the number), then text. The hero pairs the wide Call with a plain "or send a text" link; the close pairs Call with a Send a text outline button. Don't add a third channel or a duplicate to a group.

**The No Sticky Bar, One Small Pill Rule.** No full-width sticky or fixed bars or banners, ever. A single small labeled Call pill is allowed on phones only: amber, fully rounded, "Call Ed", pinned 16px from the bottom-right corner (plus the safe-area inset). It shows only after the hero call group has scrolled away and hides while the closing buttons are on screen, so it never doubles a visible Call. This is a deliberate per-site exception Ellie made on 2026-10-14 for this 40–70 audience; it does not carry to other sites.

**The Nothing Hidden Behind A Tap Rule.** No accordions, tabs, carousels, "read more" or tap-to-reveal. Every question shows its answer in full and every step shows its text. If text is worth having on the page, it is visible on arrival; if it isn't, cut it.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat with two exceptions. Aluminum and night sections use no shadows: depth comes from the change between night and aluminum, and from the seams. The driver sign is lifted because a real sign stands off its post. The Call pill is lifted because it floats over the page. Inside the road canvas, the car, trees, houses and state signs cast small offset shadows at 28–35% black. That is the canvas's own top-down lighting, not UI elevation.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Sign Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -12px rgba(0,0,0,.55), 0 2px 0 #084d2b`): guide-sign panels only. The diffuse part lifts the panel off the ground. The 2px lip is the panel's own metal edge, always sign-deep and never black.
- **Pill Float** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px -6px rgba(5,20,25,.55)`): the Call pill only, a night-tinted diffuse shadow that separates it from whatever scrolls beneath.

### Named Rules
**The Only Signs And The Pill Stand Up Rule.** Only guide-sign panels and the floating Call pill get elevation. Buttons, links, step discs, lists, question pairs and sections stay flat.

## Shapes

Corners are soft. The sign is 16px outside and 11px on the inset; the inset is a 3px sign-white border set 7px in from the panel edge, so the two radii stay concentric. Buttons are 12px. The brand link is 8px, so its focus ring hugs the lockup. Focus rings are 6px. Service bullets are 12px squares at 3px. Step numbers sit in 44px circles. The Call pill is the only fully rounded control (999px), which marks it as a different object from the in-page buttons. Section edges are never straight lines: each change of ground is a wavy edge drawn by a seam.

## Components

### Buttons
Large, direct, and only for the thumb.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (12px), 58px minimum height, 2px border, a 22px inline SVG icon (phone or chat) 10px before the label, no wrapping.
- **Call (primary):** amber fill and border, ink text. The label is two lines, left-aligned: "Call for a free quote" in Button, the number beneath in Button Number. In the close it fills the column on phones and sizes to content from 760px.
- **Call, wide (hero):** the same button made bigger for the first viewport: 66px minimum height, 22px side padding, content aligned left.
- **Send a text (ghost):** transparent fill, on-dark text, 2px border at on-dark-55. Opens `sms:` with a pre-filled message. Close only. Hidden on fine-pointer devices.
- **Hover / Focus:** hover applies only under `(hover:hover)`. Call lightens to lit amber and its border turns shield blue. The ghost's border turns shield blue and its text goes white. Focus is a 3px shield-blue outline at 3px offset. Transitions: background and border .2s, transform .15s; pressing nudges the button down 1px.

### Text Link
- **Or send a text (hero):** Text Link type in on-dark with a 2px underline at 5px offset, 48px minimum height, 4px side padding, left-aligned under the Call button. The underline turns shield blue on hover. Opens the same pre-filled `sms:`. Hidden on fine-pointer devices.

### Call Pill
The one floating element, phones only.
- **Shape:** fully rounded (999px), 56px minimum height, 20px side padding, a 20px phone icon 8px before "Call Ed" in Call Pill type. Its accessible name carries the full "Call Ed for a free quote, 732-300-3835".
- **Colour:** amber fill, ink text, Pill Float shadow.
- **Behaviour:** fixed bottom-right, 16px from the edges plus the bottom safe area. Hidden (transparent, 12px lower, not tappable) until the hero call group scrolls above the viewport, and again while the closing buttons are visible. It appears and leaves with a .25s opacity and position transition, which is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Gone at 760px and up.

### Guide Sign (signature)
The house component, now used once: "Meet your driver".
- **Structure:** a green panel (7px padding, 16px radius, Sign Lift), an inset with a 3px sign-white border at 11px radius, content in sign-white.
- **Driver sign:** inset padding 24/20/26 on phones, 40/44 wide, panel capped at 860px. Holds an uppercase Headline, a Lede in sign-white, and the Phone Number link: a 3px underline at sign-white-50 that turns shield blue on hover, a 30px phone icon, 48px minimum height. Focus outlines inside the sign are sign-white.
- **Canvas variant:** the state-line signs drawn in the road ("Welcome to / Georgia"): same green, a 1.5px sign-white inset stroke, 4px radius, Canvas Sign type, mixed case.

### Steps (How it works)
Three static numbered steps, never a carousel or a timeline animation.
- **Structure:** an ordered list, 22px apart. Each step is a 48px column holding a 44px sign-green disc with its sign-white Step Number, beside a Question-style h3 and a body paragraph (max 46ch).

### Seams (signature)
A still lane line along the top edge of the next ground.
- **Form:** an SVG with a 1200×64 viewBox stretched full width, 44px tall (64px at 760px and up), sitting in flow between bands. The fill path takes the next ground (`--next`): paper below the hero, road-band above the close. The stroke is a 3px dashed lane line (26 on, 22 off, round caps) in `--lane`.
- **Wave:** `y = 34 + 9·sin(x/190 + φ) + 5·sin(x/83 + 2φ)`, sampled every 20 units, with φ = 1.7 × seam index so no two seams match. Drawn once on load; it never animates.
- **Colour:** amber lane on both seams, because each one crosses into or out of night.

### Road Canvas (signature)
A top-down night highway band that opens the close: scenery to watch, never a control.
- **Scene:** a winding two-lane road across eight states (New Jersey to Florida, 900px of road each). Ground warms from cool green in Jersey to warmer green in Florida. Shoulder gravel, asphalt, sign-white edge lines at 80%, an amber centre dash 16/16, still reflector dots at 30% sign-white. Trees go pine to oak to palm on the way south. A house sits at each end of the trip. The packed cream car (amber and green cargo) carries an additive headlight beam. A 40px night fade covers its foot.
- **Motion:** the car drives at a steady CRUISE of 110px/s from one house to the other, pauses 2.5s, turns round and drives back. The camera and lane position ease in; nothing else in the scene moves. Direction starts southbound September to February and northbound March to August.
- **Input:** none. No drag, no steering, no hint, no `touch-action` override: the page scrolls normally over the band.
- **Running:** one shared requestAnimationFrame loop (frame time capped at 50ms), paused whenever the band is off screen. Road width 60px when the canvas is under 700px wide, 78px otherwise; devicePixelRatio capped at 2.
- **Reduced motion:** draws one still frame.

**The One Moving Thing Rule.** Only the car moves. Seams, text, illustrations and sections never animate, reveal on scroll, or loop. The only other changes on the page are state feedback (colour shifts and a 1px press) in direct response to a touch, hover or keyboard focus, and the Call pill's short fade in and out, which is switched off under reduced motion.

### Lists
- **What I do:** Title-style lines between 2px dotted guardrail rules, each led by a 12px sign-green square (3px radius).
- **Questions:** plain question/answer pairs in a block capped at 760px: a Question h3 (20px/750) above a body paragraph, with a 2px solid guardrail rule above the first pair and below every answer. Cost is answered first. Always open; no toggle, no icon.

### Navigation
- **Header:** not sticky. The brand lockup is a link back to the top: a 34px clover and "Shamrock Transportation" in Wordmark, 8px radius for its focus ring. On phones that is all the header holds.
- **Desktop (760px and up):** three in-page links (Meet your driver / How it works / Questions) in Nav Link type, then the phone number in Bar Phone type with a 20px phone icon, all on-dark, 44px minimum height. Hover adds a 2px shield-blue bottom border; at rest the border is transparent.

### Close and Footer
- **Close:** on night, under the road band. Headline (seasonal: "Heading south this winter?" / "Heading home this spring?"), a lede in dim haze, the Close Number in on-dark with a 3px on-dark-40 underline that turns shield blue on hover, the contact pair (max 520px), then the card tagline in italic body.

Links have a 2px underline at 4px offset, 44px minimum height, and take a shield-blue underline on hover. Four columns spread apart at 760px and up.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put the driver on a guide sign: green, 3px sign-white inset border, 16/11px concentric radii, Sign Lift shadow.
- **Do** keep amber for calling and the centre line. Text on amber is always ink.
- **Do** write the phone number out wherever the page asks for a call, and put it on the second line of every Call button.
- **Do** show blue only on hover (gated behind `(hover:hover)`) and focus. Use deep shield blue on aluminum and sign-white focus inside green signs.
- **Do** stitch every change of ground with a still seam whose fill is the next ground's colour.
- **Do** keep reading text at 18px or larger and buttons at 58px tall or more (the Call pill at 56px).
- **Do** show every answer and every step in full, on arrival.
- **Do** keep the car the only moving thing: run it on the shared loop, pause it off screen, and draw a still frame under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Do** write icons as inline SVG symbols that inherit `currentColor`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a second moving thing: no rolling seams, scroll reveals, marquees, looping illustrations, or extra traffic in the road.
- **Don't** make the road band interactive or capture touch on it. It is scenery; the page must scroll over it.
- **Don't** hide text behind a tap: no accordions, tabs, carousels or "read more".
- **Don't** add a full-width sticky or fixed bar or banner, ever. The one allowed floating element is the small labeled Call pill on phones.
- **Don't** put more than two contact controls in a group, or use amber for anything that isn't calling.
- **Don't** use blue at rest as a link colour, accent, or fill.
- **Don't** use #000 or near-black as a ground. Night is blue-green.
- **Don't** put a tracked uppercase label above a headline. The page has no tracked uppercase text.
- **Don't** add shadows to buttons, links, step discs, lists, question pairs or sections. Only signs and the Call pill stand up.
- **Don't** use stock fleet photos, quote forms, or trust badges.
